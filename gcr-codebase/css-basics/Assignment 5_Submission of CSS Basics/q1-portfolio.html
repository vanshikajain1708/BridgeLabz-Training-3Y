Exploring Manali

Overview Attractions Food Travel Tips

Overview

Manali is a beautiful hill station in Himachal Pradesh, known for its snow-capped mountains, lush valleys, and adventure sports.

Top Attractions

Solang Valley, Rohtang Pass, Hadimba Temple, and Old Manali are must-visit spots for every traveler.

Local Food

Try Siddu, Trout Fish, and Himachali Dham for an authentic taste of the region.

Travel Tips

Visit between October and June, carry warm clothes, and book accommodations in advance during peak season.
